About Publications Experience Education

Hello. I am an Economist at the Global Development Network (GDN), where I lead research for the Inclusivity Measurement Programme within GDN's Inclusive Digital Transformation Hub, which houses the network's work on Digital Public Infrastructure. Our work supports research and evidence on how countries design, deploy, and benefit from inclusive digital transformation.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the Geneva Graduate Institute. My research spans digital economics, migration, public finance, gender, and household behaviour in developing countries. I am a research fellow at the International Institute of Migration and Development, and I hold a Master in Development Studies from the Geneva Graduate Institute and a Bachelor in Economics from the University of Delhi.
